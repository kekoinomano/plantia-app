Promotional text (optional):
Hear how your plant's signal inspires music. Connect the saviasound sensor, choose a mood, and enjoy an ever-changing soundscape.

Description:
Hear your plant in a new way. saviasound receives the signal captured by a compatible physical sensor over Bluetooth and turns it into music.

Connect the sensor, choose from different musical moods, and adjust the volume. Watch your plant's signal change as you listen. The music can keep playing when you lock your screen.

The main feature requires a compatible saviasound physical sensor, sold separately. No account is required.
